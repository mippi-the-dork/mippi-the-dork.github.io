## Design Intent

The six major sections are the professional backbone of the website. They represent the areas a visitor should be able to evaluate without participating in the hidden game layer.

The set is intentionally small enough to be understood from the header and broad enough to contain the portfolio's different kinds of work.

## Canonical Sections

### Games

Primary destination for game design work and featured projects.

### Career

Professional history, studio experience, roles, and resume depth.

### Consulting

Public consulting work, service framing, and NDA-safe presentation.

### Crafting Play

Design writing, frameworks, educational work, and reusable design thinking.

### Tools

Unreal Engine editor tools and plugin work.

### About

Personal context, contact information, Doug, and closing identity material.

## Ordering

The order reflects the intended professional reading priority:

1. Work.
2. History.
3. Services.
4. Thinking.
5. Toolmaking.
6. Personal context.

The order should remain stable unless the portfolio's strategic goals change.

## Progression Relationship

The sections serve two purposes:

- professional information architecture,
- broad-engagement progression evidence.

The normal Finale requires broad section engagement rather than every section. Postgame completion requires all six.

The progression relationship must not change how the sections are written or accessed. A section is not a level gate.

## Visual Rhythm

Standard and alternate-background sections are interleaved to provide separation across a long single-page portfolio.

Each major section contains its own internal feature hierarchy, but the six top-level section IDs remain the stable navigation layer.

## Design Rules

- All six remain reachable from Primary Navigation.
- Content remains available at the starting rarity.
- Section content does not depend on a game unlock.
- Game interactions inside a section are optional enhancements.
- Section IDs and names should not be casually changed because many systems refer to them.
