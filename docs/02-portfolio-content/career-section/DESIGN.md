## Design Intent

The Career section compresses more than 25 years of professional work into an editorial overview that can be scanned quickly without pretending to be the full resume.

The section should communicate range while preserving a coherent career arc:

- early QA and game development,
- AAA technical and gameplay work,
- live-service and localization work,
- real-time creative technology,
- consulting and leadership,
- studio design direction and game direction.

## Content Layers

The Career section uses several layers instead of one long chronology.

### Current / Recent Role

The Studio Design Director card anchors the section in the most recent studio leadership work.

### Career Range

A compact card summarizes the trajectory from hands-on AAA and indie work into leadership.

### NDA Game Director Project

A dedicated NDA-safe card acknowledges the first Game Director role without revealing protected project information.

### Selected Studios and Companies

The studio wall provides recognizable career context and role summaries.

### Career Highlights Timeline

The timeline compresses many roles into a few meaningful eras.

### Resume Selector

The resume panel lets the visitor choose how much detail they want beyond the curated website.

## Editorial Principle

The site is curated. The resume is exhaustive.

The Career section should answer "what kind of career is this?" while the external resumes answer "what happened in detail?"

This prevents the portfolio from becoming a duplicate of a long CV.

## Visual Content Policy

Studio and IP imagery is background context, not the readable information layer.

Role, company, and date information must remain legible without relying on the artwork.

The NDA project deliberately uses obscured presentation and a classified identity hint rather than inventing public details.

## Design Rules

- Recent leadership should be easy to find.
- Early hands-on work should remain visible enough to show craft continuity.
- The career story should not suggest that leadership replaced making things.
- NDA work must be acknowledged without speculation.
- The section should not require the visitor to open an external resume to understand the overall career.
- Resume depth should remain optional.

## Progression Relationship

Career is one of the six major tracked sections.

The Long resume also hosts the Rare achievement `The Whole Story`.

The NDA identity hint hosts the Rare achievement `Classified`.

Neither achievement is required to access career information.
