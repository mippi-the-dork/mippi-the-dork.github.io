## Design Intent

The Consulting section is not a sales funnel built around generic service claims.

It should communicate how Mippi approaches design problems and show that consultation is grounded in real development constraints.

The core positioning is:

`Finding the design problem that actually matters.`

## Service Areas

The four current consulting areas are:

### Design Diagnosis

Separate symptoms from root causes and frame testable design questions.

### Systems and Progression

Review loops, rewards, economies, progression, and choice structures.

### Combat and Enemies

Evaluate verbs, enemy roles, encounter pressure, counters, and readability.

### Preproduction

Turn broad ideas into questions, prototypes, acceptance criteria, and decisions before content scale hides the problem.

## Design Rules

- Consulting copy should focus on problem-solving, not vague expertise language.
- Public project links should support credibility without implying ownership of the whole product.
- Development status must be accurate.
- Projects without public links should still be represented if they are useful examples.
- Consulting cards are content, not buttons.
- The section should not pressure the visitor with aggressive conversion UI.
