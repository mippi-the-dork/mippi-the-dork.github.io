## Design Intent

Crafting Play demonstrates that design knowledge can be externalized, taught, and reused.

The section expands the portfolio beyond project credits by showing how Mippi organizes design reasoning into frameworks, guides, workbooks, and educational material.

The central statement is:

`Design knowledge should be usable.`

## Current Destinations

Two cards lead into the broader Crafting Play work.

### Game Design Frameworks and Tools

Positions Crafting Play as a collection of practical design thinking and working materials.

### Mippi's Handbook to Crafting Play

Frames the work as a practical handbook for learning and applying game design.

Both currently lead to the Crafting Play website.

## Design Rules

- The section should feel like a professional body of knowledge, not a blog-roll.
- Emphasize practical application over abstract theory.
- External links must clearly indicate that they leave the portfolio.
- The two cards should communicate different reasons to visit even when they share a destination.
- Educational work should support the same design identity as Games and Consulting.

## Relationship to Other Content

Crafting Play is a separate major section because it represents reusable methodology rather than one project or service.

It complements:

- Games by explaining design thinking.
- Consulting by showing structured problem-solving.
- Tools by showing the same impulse to turn repeated problems into reusable systems.

## Progression Relationship

Crafting is one of the six major sections used by section-visit tracking and postgame Grand Tour completion.

No hidden achievement is required to access the external resources.

## Success Criteria

A visitor should understand that Mippi not only designs games, but also formalizes design knowledge in a way other designers can use.
