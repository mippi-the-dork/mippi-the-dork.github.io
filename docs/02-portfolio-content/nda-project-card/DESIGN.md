## Design Intent

The NDA card solves a portfolio problem: a meaningful Game Director role should not disappear from the career story simply because the project is unannounced.

The card acknowledges the experience while respecting the boundary that the actual title and protected details cannot be disclosed.

## What the Card Communicates

The card can safely state:

- this was Mippi's first Game Director role,
- it was at Mob Entertainment,
- the project remains unannounced,
- it is under NDA,
- the experience taught meaningful lessons about carrying a game from vision through production.

The card should not speculate about title, genre, characters, mechanics, release timing, or announcement plans.

## Identity Hint

The card includes a pixel-art Mob Entertainment logo and the label:

`PROJECT IDENTITY // CLASSIFIED`

This is intentionally a hint about the studio context, not a reveal of the protected project.

Clicking it can award the Rare achievement `Classified`.

The achievement turns the NDA limitation into a playful interaction without requiring the site to leak anything.

## Design Rules

- Never trade confidentiality for a more exciting portfolio card.
- The copy should be useful even if the visitor never clicks the secret.
- The logo hint should identify the public studio relationship only.
- Hidden achievement language must not imply unreleased project facts.
- The visual treatment should communicate redaction or classification without making the card unreadable.
- The card must remain respectful of future announcement changes.

## Future Update Path

When the project becomes publicly announceable, this card can be converted into a normal project or career feature.

The documentation should then record:

- what NDA restrictions were removed,
- what public details can be added,
- whether the Classified achievement remains as legacy flavor or is redesigned.
