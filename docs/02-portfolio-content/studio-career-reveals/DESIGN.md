# Studio Career Reveals - Design Documentation

**Introduced:** v3.12. **Current:** v3.14.

All **14** cards under Selected Studios & Companies open a slide-up, within-card career summary. Selecting another card closes the previous one. Visitors can dismiss by clicking the open reveal, using its Close button, or pressing Escape. These details are short introductions to distinct career contributions, not scrollable résumé pages.

**v3.14 supersedes earlier copy/layout:** each reveal has **1–3 distinct, concise bullets**, with repeated ideas removed. Typography, spacing and card minimum heights are tuned to keep content readable without internal scrollbars. Studio artwork remains **full-color** at rest, on hover, on keyboard focus and after dismissal; accent overlays, not grayscale, indicate interaction.

The first **Your Studio Here - Mippi is available for work** card combines a concise professional description with two compact horizontal link rows: **CONTACT** (Discord, LinkedIn, Email) and **RESUMES** (1 Page, Short, Long). The entire grid remains professional and navigable with mouse and keyboard.

Test all 14 individually, especially cards with 3 bullets, narrow screens and link focus. See [Technical](TECHNICAL.md) and [v3.14 QA](../../QA/V3_11_TO_V3_14_REGRESSION_QA.md).
