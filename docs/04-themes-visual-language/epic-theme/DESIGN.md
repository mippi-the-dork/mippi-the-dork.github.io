## Design Intent

Epic is the first moment where the page should feel like it has crossed into a new state.

The player has found all 10 original Rare Signals.

The visual reward should therefore be obvious.

Epic is not only a recolor.

It increases:

- saturation,
- glow,
- motion presence,
- visual energy,
- layered accent use.

## Visual Identity

Epic uses:

- violet as the main rarity color,
- hot pink as the emotional high-energy accent,
- cyan as the technical complement,
- dark plum and graphite backgrounds.

The three-color combination matters.

Pure purple would feel too monotone.

The hot pink creates energy.

Cyan preserves the technical language established by Rare.

## Emotional Tone

Epic should feel:

- celebratory,
- energized,
- overclocked,
- more expressive,
- still technical.

It is the point where the hidden game layer is no longer a tiny secret.

## Site-Wide Transformation

Epic affects:

- page background,
- header,
- Hero,
- Three.js scenes,
- panels,
- headings,
- links,
- Ledger chrome,
- Console chrome,
- pickups,
- fixed HUD,
- secondary effects,
- cursor aura.

Selected heading treatments use gradients that blend white, violet, cyan, and pink.

## Completion Cursor Aura

Epic introduces a visible cursor aura and sparks.

This is a deliberate expression of having completed the first major discovery tier.

The aura is presentation only.

It should not become the actual authoritative pointer hitbox.

## Airspace Relationship

Epic progression also opens airborne systems.

That gameplay unlock is related to Epic progression, but not to Epic visual selection.

A player can switch the visual theme back to Rare and airborne systems remain available.

## Semantic Exceptions

Some systems preserve their own colors.

Examples:

- Blueprint node semantics.
- HAL red eye.
- hostile combat readability.
- rarity-owned Rare and Legendary entries.

Epic should theme the world around those systems without destroying their meaning.

## Design Rules

- Epic should be clearly recognizable within one screen.
- Hot pink should remain an accent, not replace violet as the entire palette.
- Cyan should continue to communicate technical/high-energy surfaces.
- Avoid leaving accidental Unreal-blue UI in Epic.
- Do not recolor Rare achievement entries purple.
- Do not recolor Legendary achievement entries purple.
- Epic gameplay remains active in any selected theme after unlock.

## Success Criteria

Epic succeeds when the player feels that finishing the first discovery layer materially changed the site's presentation and unlocked a new stage of play.
