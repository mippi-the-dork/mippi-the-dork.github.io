## Design Intent

Legendary is the highest global rarity theme.

It should feel unmistakably more prestigious than Epic without becoming visually gaudy or abandoning the technical identity of the site.

The key palette relationship is:

- warm rarity color,
- cool technical complement.

Gold and amber communicate rarity.

Teal and cyan keep the system grounded in the same technical language established by Rare.

## Visual Identity

Legendary uses:

- gold,
- amber,
- orange,
- teal,
- cyan support,
- warm dark graphite.

The site should not become uniformly yellow.

The teal complement is important for:

- readability,
- technical highlights,
- contrast,
- continuity with the prior themes.

## Emotional Tone

Legendary should feel:

- rare,
- prestigious,
- strange,
- confident,
- advanced.

It represents that the player has gone beyond the first completion layer and successfully validated a Wish.

## Material Upgrade

Legendary changes more than color.

Examples include:

- richer heading gradients,
- stronger brand treatment,
- additional Hero geometry,
- gold/teal pickup treatment,
- enhanced Finale-ready Mippi logo cue,
- gold/teal HUD attention states,
- Legendary cursor weapon eligibility,
- Legendary Music Player theme.

## Hero Upgrade

Legendary Hero adds:

- wireframe shell,
- additional rings,
- orbiting shards.

This makes Legendary feel structurally upgraded rather than simply palette-swapped.

## Complementary Teal

Teal replaces many leftover cool accents that would otherwise remain blue and fight the warm rarity.

This is especially important in:

- borders,
- link treatments,
- tool status accents,
- camera visualizer elements,
- pickups,
- Doug effects,
- console support colors.

## Finale Attention

The Mippi header icon must continue to pulse when the normal Finale is ready.

A prior regression allowed the cue in Rare and Epic but effectively lost it in Legendary.

Legendary now has its own explicit gold/teal Finale-ready animation.

## Design Rules

- Gold is the dominant rarity signal.
- Teal is the primary cool support color.
- Avoid residual Epic pink unless it is a semantic exception.
- Avoid residual Rare blue unless it is a semantic exception.
- Legendary should add material richness, not only saturation.
- Rarity-owned Epic and Rare components retain their own rarity colors.
- Legendary gameplay remains unlocked even when another visual theme is selected.

## Success Criteria

Legendary succeeds when the player immediately understands that this is the rarest world state and can still read the page comfortably for professional use.
