## Design Intent

Air Superiority is the arcade mastery achievement.

The normal Boss is enough for normal Finale eligibility.

The Super Boss is intentionally reserved for the deeper postgame completion tier.

This separates:

- broad engagement,
- mastery.

## Condition

Defeat the Super UFO Boss.

## Historical Recognition

A Super Boss defeat before postgame reveal should count later.

The player should not be forced to defeat it twice.

## Design Rules

- Normal Boss does not qualify.
- Super Boss must reach its real victory state.
- DEV forcing can be used for QA but not natural player progression.
