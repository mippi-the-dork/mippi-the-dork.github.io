## Design Intent

Another Castle recognizes completion of the repeated Castle loop.

The title turns the familiar "another castle" structure into an explicit postgame milestone.

## Condition

Reach the fourth Castle completion.

The click-to-claim fireworks reward is a separate agency step.

## Historical Recognition

If the fourth Castle was completed before postgame reveal, the site should remember it.

## Design Rules

- The player should not need to repeat Castle progression.
- Claiming fireworks can remain separate from achievement qualification.
- Castle completion should persist.
