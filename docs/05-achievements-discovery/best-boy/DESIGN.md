## Design Intent

Best Boy makes Doug more than a single easter egg.

The player must interact repeatedly.

That matters because Doug later becomes:

- a Coolness progression source,
- a Bone system,
- a command-state character,
- a Wish result participant.

The achievement is the first step toward understanding that Doug is a recurring system.

## Qualifying Interaction

Repeated Doug interaction qualifies after the intended pet threshold.

The exact threshold should be high enough that one accidental click does not unlock the achievement immediately.

## Design Rules

- Doug interaction should always have positive feedback.
- Repeated clicks should produce variation rather than identical response.
- The achievement should feel earned through affection/curiosity, not grinding.
- Later Coolness systems should build on the same interaction identity.

## Success Criteria

The player understands that Doug is a persistent character with more depth than a static About card.
