## Design Intent

Classified turns the limitations of NDA-safe presentation into a discovery opportunity.

The player learns that the card has one extra layer without receiving any confidential project detail.

## Qualifying Interaction

Activate the NDA identity hint.

## Confidentiality Rule

The achievement may acknowledge:

- Mob Entertainment,
- unannounced project,
- Game Director role,
- classified identity.

It must not reveal:

- title,
- genre,
- mechanic,
- characters,
- release information,
- protected production detail.

## Design Rules

- The secret must remain NDA-safe.
- Accessibility text must also remain NDA-safe.
- Console output must remain NDA-safe.
- Unlocking the achievement must not reveal new confidential copy.

## Success Criteria

The player feels rewarded for curiosity while the portfolio remains fully compliant with confidentiality constraints.
