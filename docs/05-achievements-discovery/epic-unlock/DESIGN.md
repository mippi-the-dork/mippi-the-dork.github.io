# Epic Unlock - Design Documentation

**Hierarchy:** Documentation Home > Achievements and Discovery > Epic Unlock > Design

**Feature summary:** Unlocks the Epic progression tier once all 10 original Rare Signals have been found.

## Design Intent

Epic Unlock is the transition from hidden discovery into obvious systemic play.

The player has demonstrated enough curiosity to understand that the page contains a game layer.

The reward should therefore be meaningful.

## What Changes

Epic unlock can introduce:

- Epic theme availability,
- stronger visual presentation,
- new airborne/gameplay opportunities,
- additional progression guidance,
- deeper systems such as Wish setup and arcade escalation.

The unlock should feel like entering a second chapter.

## Design Rules

- Trigger exactly at 10/10 original Rare Signals.
- Do not require the player to select Epic visual theme for Epic gameplay.
- Explain that something changed.
- Preserve all Rare content and achievements.
- Avoid immediately stacking too many new events on top of the unlock moment.

## Success Criteria

The player recognizes that the first achievement set was not the entire game and is naturally pushed toward the next layer.
