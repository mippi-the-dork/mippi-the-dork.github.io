## Design Intent

Full Hearts represents completion of the Heart collectible progression.

The player should receive credit for reaching 5/5.

The separate click-to-claim reward interaction is player agency around the payoff, not a reason to deny the completion achievement.

## Condition

All five Hearts collected.

## Design Rules

- Heart collection can happen before postgame reveal.
- Historical 5/5 should count.
- The achievement should not require recollecting Hearts after claiming the reward.
