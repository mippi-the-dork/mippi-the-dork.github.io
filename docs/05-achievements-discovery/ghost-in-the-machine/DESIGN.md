## Design Intent

Ghost in the Machine is the most explicitly ephemeral Rare Signal.

It teaches the player that not every discovery is permanently waiting in a static card.

Something can appear briefly in the ambient layer and reward attention.

## Experience Goal

The Signal should feel surprising rather than frustrating.

It can be rare, but anti-drought logic should prevent the player from waiting indefinitely once the system is relevant.

## Qualifying Interaction

The player must click the transient signal before its lifetime expires.

## Design Rules

- The object must be visually distinguishable from noninteractive ambient decoration.
- Its lifetime must be long enough to react.
- Missing it should have low cost.
- It can return later.
- It should not block normal portfolio interaction.

## Success Criteria

The achievement creates a memorable "I saw that" moment without becoming a hard random-number gate.
