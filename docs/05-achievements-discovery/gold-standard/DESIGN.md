## Design Intent

Gold Standard recognizes completion of the Wish progression that unlocks Legendary rarity.

It is a progression milestone rather than a requirement to keep the Legendary visual theme selected.

## Condition

Legendary progression unlocked.

## Design Rules

- Theme selection does not matter.
- Once Legendary is unlocked, Gold Standard remains valid permanently.
- Switching back to Rare does not revoke it.
