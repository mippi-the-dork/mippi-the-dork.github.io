# Maximum Cool - Design Documentation

**Hierarchy:** Documentation Home > Achievements and Discovery > Maximum Cool > Design

**Implementation baseline:** Mippi Portfolio v2.78

**Feature summary:** Rewards having maxed the Coolness meter and activated Cool Mode at least once.

## Design Intent

Maximum Cool represents full engagement with Doug's morale progression.

It is not enough to merely meet Doug.

The player must take the Coolness system to its maximum state.

## Condition

Coolness reaches its cap and the maximum state has occurred.

## Historical Recognition

The site remembers that Coolness was maxed even if the current session meter later changes.

This protects completion across session boundaries.

## Design Rules

- Do not require postgame reveal before maxing Coolness.
- Previously maxed state must count.
- The achievement should represent the milestone, not current temporary meter value.
