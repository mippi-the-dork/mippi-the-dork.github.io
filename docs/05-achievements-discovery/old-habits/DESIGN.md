## Design Intent

Old Habits is the one deliberately classic secret-code achievement.

It recognizes a familiar piece of game culture and rewards players who try it instinctively.

## Qualifying Input

The standard Konami sequence qualifies.

The sequence should be recognized globally without making normal keyboard use unreliable.

## Design Rules

- Typing in normal inputs must remain safe.
- The sequence should not interfere with the Dev Console.
- Repeated completion should not duplicate the achievement.
- The secret should remain playful and optional.

## Success Criteria

A player who tries the classic sequence receives an immediate recognition moment without disrupting normal page behavior.
