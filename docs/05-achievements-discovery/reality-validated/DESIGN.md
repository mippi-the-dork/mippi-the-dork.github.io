## Design Intent

Reality Validated acknowledges completion of one of the site's most involved progression puzzles.

The player must:

- obtain a Shooting Star charge,
- encounter the broken Wish path,
- repair BP_WishManager,
- submit a validated Wish.

The achievement recognizes successful completion of that chain.

## Condition

At least one validated Wish succeeds.

The content of the Wish does not need to be a specific branch.

## Design Rules

- Invalid Wish attempts do not count.
- Merely holding a charge does not count.
- Merely repairing the Blueprint does not count.
- Any successful validated Wish qualifies.
