## Design Intent

Second Perspective rewards engaging with one of the portfolio's most meaningful interactive design explanations.

The achievement is not attached to a random toy.

It reinforces the actual Heavy Water thesis: camera ownership changes gameplay perspective.

## Qualifying Interaction

The player should meaningfully use the Perspective Visualizer rather than merely scroll past it.

Selecting second-person perspective is the strongest expression of that interaction.

## Design Rules

- The visualizer remains professional content first.
- Achievement feedback should not obscure the visualizer.
- The achievement should reward real mode interaction.
- The player should not need to understand the achievement before using the demo.

## Success Criteria

The player learns something about Heavy Water and receives a Signal for doing so.

## v3.05–v3.10 Current Specification

**Current v3.06+ condition:** Only selecting **2nd Person** in the Project Heavy Water Perspective Visualizer grants Second Perspective. Switching to 1st or 3rd does not grant it. The hint refers to the option beyond first/third-person, and the solution explicitly says switch to **2nd Person**.
