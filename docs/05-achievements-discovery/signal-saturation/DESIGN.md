## Design Intent

Signal Saturation is the postgame acknowledgement of completing the original discovery chapter.

It does not ask the player to do something new.

It recognizes that the first chapter is complete.

## Condition

All 10 Rare Signals found.

## Design Rule

If postgame is revealed after the player already has 10/10, Signal Saturation should resolve immediately.

The player should never be asked to repeat Rare discoveries.
