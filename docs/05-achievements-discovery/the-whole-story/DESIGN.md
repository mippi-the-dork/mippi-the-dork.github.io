# The Whole Story - Design Documentation

**Hierarchy:** Documentation Home > Achievements and Discovery > The Whole Story > Design

**Implementation baseline:** Mippi Portfolio v2.78

**Feature summary:** Rewards choosing the Long resume rather than only viewing the shorter career summaries.

## Design Intent

The Whole Story rewards a visitor who explicitly asks for the most detailed career history.

It fits the discovery set because the Resume Selector already offers different levels of depth.

The achievement does not manipulate the visitor into opening the Long resume.

It simply recognizes the choice.

## Qualifying Interaction

Clicking the Long resume option qualifies.

## Design Rules

- Long remains a legitimate professional link first.
- The achievement should not delay or block the resume opening.
- Shorter resume choices remain fully valid professional paths.
- No resume length should be visually framed as "wrong."

## Success Criteria

The player gets a small reward for deliberately asking for the complete career story.
