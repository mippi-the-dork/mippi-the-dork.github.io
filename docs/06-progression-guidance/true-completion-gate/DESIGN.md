## Design Intent

The portfolio has two endings on purpose.

The first is the authored normal Finale.

The second is true completion.

These should not be collapsed into one giant requirement list.

## Why Two Gates Exist

A normal ending should be reachable by a player who has meaningfully engaged with the site.

If the Finale required every optional system, many players would never see the authored ending.

The postgame exists for players who actively want mastery.

This creates a healthier structure:

### Normal Finale

Broad engagement.

### True Completion

Comprehensive mastery.

## Normal Finale Standard

The player must demonstrate meaningful interaction with the major systems, but not complete every system.

The broad gate includes things like:

- Rare discovery complete,
- Legendary unlocked,
- validated Wish,
- Boss victory,
- Heart reward,
- some Shop engagement,
- most major sections visited.

## Postgame Standard

After the Finale, nine Epic achievements define mastery:

- Signal Saturation,
- Maximum Cool,
- Gold Standard,
- Reality Validated,
- Full Hearts,
- Buy the Store,
- Another Castle,
- Grand Tour,
- Air Superiority.

## Legendary Capstone

True completion requires:

- all nine Epic achievements,
- Finale seen.

The resulting Legendary achievement is:

`100 Percent Complete`

## Reward

The Music Player unlocks permanently until reset.

This reward is intentionally persistent.

The player has finished the game layer, so the reward should remain available as a new way to enjoy the site rather than becoming another temporary event.

## No Theme Dependency

The true completion gate does not require the player to display Legendary theme.

Legendary progression must be unlocked, but the active theme can be:

- Rare,
- Epic,
- Legendary.

## Cheat Policy

Cheat can explain incomplete Epic achievements.

Cheat ignores the Legendary capstone.

The capstone should be the self-evident result of finishing the visible mastery list.

## Design Rules

- Normal Finale remains accessible before 100 percent completion.
- Postgame goals stay hidden before Finale reveal.
- Historical qualifying actions should count.
- True completion is persistent.
- Music Player reward is persistent.
- Full reset is the only intended way to remove the reward.
- Missing music assets should not invalidate the achievement.

## Success Criteria

Players who want closure can reach the Finale without exhausting every system, while completionists still have a meaningful final mastery path afterward.
