## Design Intent

The Game Wish is a monkey-paw interpretation of a common developer desire:

`I wish my game was successful.`

The Wish grants exactly that.

The game becomes enormously successful.

The player simply forgot to specify that they personally receive the revenue.

## Success Fantasy

The dashboard rapidly escalates:

- copies sold,
- current players,
- peak CCU,
- reviews,
- wishlists,
- global chart position,
- gross revenue.

The first half should feel genuinely exciting.

The joke works better if the apparent success feels real before the financial catch lands.

## Monkey-Paw Turn

The bottom of the dashboard reveals:

- developer royalty: `$0.00`,
- payout destination: `MIPPI`.

The final fine print reminds the player:

`You wished for your game to be successful. You did not specify revenue participation.`

## Final Result

The final state reinforces the absurdity with:

- Mippi retirement line,
- Compensation: Exposure badge.

## Player Agency

The dashboard remains on screen after the final result.

The player dismisses it with:

`ACCEPT SUCCESS`

## Design Rules

- The game must actually become wildly successful.
- The joke is contract interpretation, not failure.
- Developer payout remains exactly zero.
- The result should stay readable after the animation ends.
- The close action appears only after the final punchline.
- The sequence does not alter real Shop Rupees or browser data.

## Success Criteria

The player experiences the thrill of impossible success before realizing the Wish subsystem interpreted the financial details with malicious precision.
