## Design Intent

The Money Wish is a short visual joke rather than a new economy system.

The player asks for money.

The site confirms that the payout has entered:

`trickle-down economics`.

The money will arrive any decade now.

The immediate payoff is a large burst of visual dollar coins.

## Keyword Family

The branch recognizes several common ways to ask for money so the joke is not dependent on one exact phrase.

Examples include:

- `$`
- money
- bucks
- dollars
- cash
- USD
- moolah
- payday

## Economy Separation

The visual coins are not Rupees.

They do not increase:

- wallet,
- Shop purchasing power,
- real-world money.

This is important because the gag is about not actually receiving the money.

## Presentation

The coins erupt from the visible Console region when possible.

They deliberately travel beyond the Console silhouette so the effect can be seen even though the Console is rendered above them.

## Design Rules

- The payout remains a visual joke.
- Do not credit Rupees.
- Use broad money-keyword matching.
- Coins remove themselves automatically.
- The Console can remain open during the effect.
- Active rarity can recolor the visual effect without changing its meaning.

## Success Criteria

The player gets an immediate absurd visual payout while mechanically receiving exactly the amount promised by the joke.
