03 // DESIGN CONSULTATION

Finding the design problem that actually matters.

Consultation for indie teams focused on design clarity, systems, combat, progression, player experience, and reducing expensive ambiguity before it becomes production debt.

01

Design Diagnosis

Separate symptoms from root causes, then frame the design problem in a way the team can actually test.

02

Systems & Progression

Review loops, rewards, progression, economies, and choice structures for clarity, depth, and unnecessary complexity.

03

Combat & Enemies

Evaluate player verbs, enemy roles, encounter pressure, readability, counters, and the space between challenge and friction.

04

Preproduction

Turn big ideas into concrete questions, prototypes, acceptance criteria, and decisions before content scale hides the problems.
